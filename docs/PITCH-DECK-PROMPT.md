# Lovable prompt: decID pitch deck

Paste everything below the line into Lovable.

Build a polished, presentation-ready **investor pitch deck web app** for a startup called **decID**: 11 full-screen slides, navigated with the arrow keys, clicks or swipes, with a slide counter and a "present" (full-screen) button. It's for a 5-minute class pitch, so every slide must read in about 25 seconds.

**Design direction (important):**
- No PowerPoint look and no generic bullet templates.
- Apple-keynote simplicity:
  - one idea per slide;
  - huge type (headlines 64–96 px, tight letter-spacing, bold), lots of white space;
  - a neutral background (#fbfbfd) with near-black text (#1d1d1f);
  - one accent colour, #0066cc;
  - success green #1f7a3d, sparingly.
- Font: Inter or the system SF font.
- Numbers as giant figures with small captions.
- Simple line icons (Lucide).
- Subtle fade/slide transitions.
- Visual metaphors instead of text where possible.
- Responsive, and readable on a projector.

**Slides and content (use this copy, tightened if needed):**

1. **Title.** "decID". Tagline: **"Prove you're 21. Nothing else."** Subtitle: "Reusable, private age and eligibility verification." Small footer: [Presenter name] · BUAD 301. Visual: a phone showing a card that reads "21+ ✓" next to a crossed-out driver's licence.

2. **The problem.** Headline: "To prove one fact, you hand over everything."
   - Visual: a driver's licence with name, address, birth date and ID number all highlighted, with an arrow to a database icon labelled "honeypot".
   - Three stat cards:
     - "~70,000 government IDs exposed in Discord's Oct 2025 age-check breach";
     - "79% of Americans worry about privacy when verifying age (2026)";
     - "52% abandon a purchase when asked for a selfie check (UK, 2025)".

3. **Why now.** A timeline:
   - Jul 2025: UK Online Safety Act age checks take effect;
   - Jun 2025: US Supreme Court upholds Texas age-verification law;
   - 2025: driver's licences in Apple and Google Wallet, and browsers ship the standard that lets websites ask a phone's wallet for an ID;
   - alcohol delivery laws require checks at sale and delivery.

   Headline: "Checks are now mandatory. The tools to do them privately just arrived."

4. **The solution.** Headline: "Verify once. Answer with one tap."
   - Three-step visual: (1) Get your Pass from the ID in your phone's wallet → (2) A business asks "21+?" → (3) It gets "Yes" plus a signed receipt, never your name, birth date or photo.
   - Small line: "Works online, at delivery and at the door."

5. **Product demo.** Headline: "See it work."
   - Mock screenshots in device frames:
     - a phone consent sheet listing "Requested: 21 or older · Not shared: name, birth date, address, ID number, photo";
     - a venue door screen in big green "OK TO ENTER" with a 4-letter code "K3V3" shown on both screens;
     - a business dashboard.
   - Button: "Live prototype" linking to [prototype link].

6. **Why it's different.** A 2×3 grid of short feature tiles:
   - "One fact, not your whole ID";
   - "Signed receipts on a blockchain: proof even decID can't rewrite";
   - "Nothing stored, nothing to breach";
   - "Fraud alerts: passed-back, fake and borrowed Passes";
   - "Face ID unlock";
   - "Compliance assistant: 'Was order #4821 age-checked?' Proven instantly."

7. **Market.** Three nested circles:
   - **TAM $44.2B**: digital identity solutions, 2025, 20% CAGR to $132B by 2031 (MarketsandMarkets);
   - **SAM ≈ $1.8B**: age-verification software, 16.5% CAGR (Marketintelo);
   - **SOM ≈ $2.4M ARR** in 3 years: US alcohol and cannabis delivery plus 21+ venues.

   Footnote with sources.

8. **Competition.** A 2×2 positioning chart. X axis "Shares only one fact → Shares the whole ID"; Y axis "Gives the business proof → No proof".
   - decID alone in the top-left (one fact plus proof).
   - Yoti / Persona / Veriff (ID upload plus selfie): bottom-right.
   - TruAge (licence scan at the register): bottom-middle.
   - Apple / Google Wallet ID: one fact but no business proof, bottom-left.
   - Bouncer looking at a plastic ID: bottom-right.

   Caption: "We build on wallet IDs, and add the proof businesses need."

9. **Business model.** Big numbers:
   - "$0.05–$0.15 per check (vs ~$1+ for ID-upload checks)";
   - "Compliance plan ~$150/month: receipts, auditor export, fraud alerts, assistant";
   - "Issuers paid per Pass".

   Beachhead: "Alcohol & cannabis delivery and 21+ venues: a legal check at every transaction."

10. **Traction and roadmap.**
    - Built:
      - working prototype;
      - 170+ automated tests and 68 security attacks blocked;
      - ID from the phone wallet;
      - blockchain-anchored receipts;
      - fraud alerts, Face ID and the compliance assistant;
      - health-record demo.
    - Next (timeline):
      - 1. pilot with one delivery retailer and one bar;
      - 2. real issuer and wallet partnerships;
      - 3. security audit;
      - 4. expand to vapes, tickets, car rental and health clearances.

11. **The ask / close.** Headline: "Prove you're 21. Nothing else."
    - Three asks: "Pilot partners (bars, delivery apps)", "Issuer partners (banks, DMVs)", "Feedback and mentorship".
    - Contact: [email]. Show a QR code linking to [prototype link].
